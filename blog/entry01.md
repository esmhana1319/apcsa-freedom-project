### Tools

Currently, the tools that i am tinkering with are TensorFlow and OpenCV, and the other two i plan to tinker with in the next two weeks are MediaPipe and Swift. 

TensorFlow: so far, ive learnt this tool is mainly focused on developing an AI with its own separate data set. For my project ill be using this ai to learn how to detect things via the camera; this is where media pipe and open cv will play in later on. 

OpenCV and MediaPipe: These tools focus on Computer vision, and detecting things with it. So far with what ive learnt in OpenCV, is that this specific tool can track objects and deploy ai for machine learning. MediaPipe is where this machine learning also takes place and is arguably better in detecting according to some. Depending on what works, i might either use both or interchange one for the other.

Swift: this tool is specifically used for making apps on IOS. What id like to do intially is incorporate the detecting feature of my tool here so that any input from the ai is sent to my phone via an app. 

### Freedom project idea

My final freedom project idea is a catcam, which can view my cats activity at when im not home and give me insight on how they are. I want to do this by making my own ai and feeding it a dataset to help it differentiate what good and bad behavior look like, and when to send notifications.
